The [marketplace](/marketplace) lists moo's built-in plugins today. Community submissions are next.

## Until then

Share the compiled `.tishc` and tell people to drop it in their `MOO_PLUGINS` folder. Tier A plugins are sandboxed, so this is safe to try.

## What's coming

- **Manifest in `moo.json`**, so the store can read commands, hotkeys, preferences and AI tools without running code.
- **Signed packages**: a `.mooplugin` archive signed with ed25519.
- **Views**: list, detail and form components rendered natively.
- **Permissions per command**, checked on every host call.

Want to be first in? [Open an issue](https://github.com/moomoi/moo/issues) with a link to your plugin.
