Type a calculation in the root search and the answer appears at the top. <kbd>Return</kbd> copies it.

## Math

| Type | Get |
| --- | --- |
| `2^10` | 1,024 |
| `15% of 80` | 12 |
| `120 + 15%` | 138 |
| `sqrt(2)` | 1.41421… |

`×`, `÷` and `−` work as well as `*`, `/` and `-`.

## Units

`5 km in mi`, `3 cups to ml`, `70f`. A bare temperature like `70f` converts to the other scale. Length, mass, volume, temperature, data and more are covered.

## Currency

`100 usd to eur`, `€50 in $`. Rates are the European Central Bank's daily reference rates.

## Number bases

`255 in hex`, `0xff`.

## Time zones

`time in tokyo`, `tokyo time`, `3pm pst to cet`, `15:30 london in new york`. Zones come from macOS's own database, so daylight saving is always right.

> **Tip** The **Unit Converter** plugin adds a list of every unit conversion, so you can pick the one you want to copy.
