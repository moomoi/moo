moo keeps a history of what you copy. Open it with <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>4</kbd> in the launcher, or search for **Clipboard History**.

- Type to filter.
- <kbd>Return</kbd> puts the entry back on the clipboard.
- **Clear Clipboard History** empties it.

> **Tip** Give it its own hotkey so it opens straight from any app. In Settings › Hotkeys and Aliases, or from the terminal: `moo hotkey add cmd+shift+v moo:clipboard`

## Privacy

moo keeps text only, in memory, and never writes it to disk or sends it anywhere. Quitting moo clears it. Passwords copied from password managers are skipped, since they mark them as concealed.
